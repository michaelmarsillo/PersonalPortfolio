# Fragrance source notes

Collection supplied by Michael; product spelling, concentrations, images, and release dates checked October 1, 2026. Ratings and personal thoughts come only from Michael and are added as he supplies them.

Images are stored locally in `public/images/archive/fragrance/`. Most are transparent PNG bottle-only catalog photos from My Perfume Shop, with Dr. Squatch and Easycosmetic photos for Fireside Bourbon and Ana Abiyedh White. After Effect is a transparent cutout made with the built-in imagegen tool from the Justmylook catalog photo. Burberry London for Men uses a transparent imagegen cutout of Perfume Gallery's dark bottle with black cap and brown glass, replacing the silver-cap photo at Michael's request. All images were visually checked for the correct bottle and concentration; packaging can change between editions.

The One and Profondo use their original EDP bottle designs. Ameer Al Oudh Intense Oud and Profondo EDP were confirmed by Michael. Ana Abiyedh (White) has no displayed release year because a reliable date could not be established.

Every public fragrance source link uses its matching Fragrantica encyclopedia page, checked October 1, 2026. The panel labels this link `Fragrantica`. The original product pages and image credits below are retained separately for provenance; the bottle images are unchanged. Profondo links to the original 2020 EDP entry matching the current bottle photo, and Eros links to the original EDT entry. These links are not claims of endorsement or image licensing.
